Trustroots is a community for travellers who want to meet, share and host each other.

This native Android preview lets Trustroots members:

* Sign in to their account
* Explore circles and manage circle membership
* Search for hosts on a map and by username
* View and edit profiles, including pictures and hosting details
* Read and send messages

The app uses OpenStreetMap tiles for its host map. Recent map results, messages and profiles have a limited offline cache.

This is a preview under active development. Some features available on the Trustroots website are not yet available in the Android app. An internet connection is needed to sign in and refresh information.
