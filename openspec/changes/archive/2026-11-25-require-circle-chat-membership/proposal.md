## Why

Circle chats should be available only to members of the corresponding circle.
Volunteering chat is open to eligible signed-in members.

## What Changes

- Require membership to read or post in the Hackers, Hitchhikers and Radio web rooms.
- Explain how to join each circle from its chat.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `mixed-circle-chat`: Require membership to access circle rooms.
- `hackers-chat`: Describe the membership requirement.
