# Open hosting results after choosing a city

## Why

The Results tab lets members browse offers without opening map pins. Choosing a city still leaves members on the map or Filters tab, and the list depends on the size of their map viewport.

## What changes

- Open the Results tab directly when a member chooses a city in place search, including when they submit with Enter.
- List hosting offers within the selected city's geocoded area using the existing visibility and member filters.
- Leave ordinary map browsing available from the city results.

## Impact

Web search reuses the existing offer search and detail endpoints.
