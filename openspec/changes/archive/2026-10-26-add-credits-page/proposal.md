## Why

Trustroots needs a discoverable home for third-party attribution without a prominent notice on maps.

## What Changes

- Add a public credits page and link to it from the footer and the approximate-location map notice.
- Credit GeoLite2, map providers, contributors and photographers while retaining map and photo credits in place.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `public-access`: Visitors can find and read product credits.

## Impact

Public routes, footer and map attribution presentation.
