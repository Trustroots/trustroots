## Why

Help members find people they know who are still active on Trustroots and see where fellow circle members offer accommodation.

## What Changes

- Show a signed-in homepage section with recently active contacts and public recommenders.
- Show approximate hosting pins for visible connections regardless of their activity.
- Show coarse activity and links to the approximate hosting map on circle member cards when applicable.
- Keep activity timestamps out of responses and apply existing visibility, block and offer access rules.

## Impact

- Affects pages, tribes and users.
- Adds a signed-in read endpoint and optional fields to circle discovery. No data migration or deployment configuration is needed.
