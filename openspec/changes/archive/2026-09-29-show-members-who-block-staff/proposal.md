# Show members who block staff

## Why

Administrators need to know which members have blocked any administrator or Welcome team member when providing support. Welcome team members need to see who blocked their own account.

## What changes

- Add a role-protected support page that groups blocking members by staff account for administrators and shows only the signed-in account for Welcome team members.
- Restrict access to administrators and Welcome team members and return only public member identifiers and names.

## Compatibility

This is additive and requires no data migration. Existing block relationships remain unchanged.
