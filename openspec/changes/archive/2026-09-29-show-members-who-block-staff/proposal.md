# Show members who block staff

## Why

Administrators and Welcome team members need to know which members have blocked their staff account when providing support.

## What changes

- Add a role-protected support page that lists members whose block list contains the signed-in staff member.
- Restrict access to administrators and Welcome team members and return only public member identifiers and names.

## Compatibility

This is additive and requires no data migration. Existing block relationships remain unchanged.
