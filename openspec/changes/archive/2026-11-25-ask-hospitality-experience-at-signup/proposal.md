# Ask about hospitality exchange experience at signup

## Why

New members may have different levels of experience hosting and staying with people they have never met. Asking about each role separately lets them describe both.

## What changes

- Ask separate, optional hosting and staying questions during web and Android signup.
- Offer the same four answers for both questions, including “No experience yet”.
- Store provided answers privately alongside the new account. Continue accepting signups that omit either answer.
