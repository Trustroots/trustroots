## Why

Featured homepage updates should credit authors using their Trustroots NIP-05 usernames as plain text.

## What Changes

- Resolve a featured note's author username through Trustroots' public NIP-05 identity rules.
- Display the username without a link and omit author attribution when no eligible username is available.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `nostr-integration`: Change how featured notes display author attribution.

## Impact

- Public homepage updates and the featured note admin list.
