# Support cannot change their own Experiences

## Why

A support member who is part of an Experience should not use the review tools to change it.

## What Changes

- Deny link issuing, approval, and rejection when a support member wrote the Experience or it was written about them.
- Keep member submission available through a link issued by someone else.
- Let administrators handle Experiences on their own profile.
- Explain the restriction in search results and the review queue.

## Impact

Affects the Experience change review API, queue, and regression tests. Existing requests need no migration.
