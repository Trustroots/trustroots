## Why

Greeters need the Acquisition stories page to open on members with visible profiles who have yet to be assigned. A shadowbanned member can have a public profile flag even though their profile is hidden from other members.

## What Changes

- Enable Unassigned only and select Visible when the page opens.
- Treat shadowbanned members as Hidden when displaying, filtering and sorting acquisition stories.
- Explain the Hidden category alongside the visibility selector.

## Impact

- Affects the Acquisition stories view for authorised staff.
