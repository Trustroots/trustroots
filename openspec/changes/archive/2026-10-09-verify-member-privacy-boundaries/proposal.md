# Verify member privacy boundaries

## Why

Reduce account takeover, data disclosure and abuse risk at existing Trustroots boundaries.

## What Changes

Add anonymous regression fixtures and a cross-surface privacy matrix for anonymous, member, blocked, suspended and administrator access. Cover profile, avatar, search, private messages and caching where applicable. Preserve intended policy and all existing end-to-end coverage.

## Impact

Affected modules: users, core, configuration and tests. Preserve client and server coverage thresholds and existing end-to-end tests. Document deployment and compatibility implications alongside implementation.
