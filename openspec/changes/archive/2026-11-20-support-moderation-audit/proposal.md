## Why

Support volunteers investigating member reports need to apply account restrictions. Administrators need to find those actions in the audit log without their own entries obscuring the results.

## What Changes

- Add shadowban and suspension controls to support member pages with server-side permission checks.
- Add audit filters for shadowban and suspension requests and a default-on checkbox to hide the signed-in viewer's actions.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `admin-moderation`: Allow support moderation and filter audit history.

## Impact

Support member pages, moderation permissions, and administrator audit history.
