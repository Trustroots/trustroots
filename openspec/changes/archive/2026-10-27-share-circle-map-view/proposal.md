## Why

Visitors switching between circle maps lose the area they were exploring.

## What changes

- Remember the chosen circle map centre and zoom for the browser session and restore them on either circle map.
- Keep the initial fit to results when no view has been chosen.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `hitchhiking`: Shared circle map navigation retains the visitor's view between circles.

## Impact

The shared circle map component and its tests.
