## Why

Members can lose a hosting offer draft when they leave the editor. The single-page form also needs an easy way to save after entering a location, especially on a phone.

## What Changes

- Ask members before leaving the editor when their changes are unsaved.
- Keep section links and successful saves free from prompts.
- Place the map under availability and description next to them on desktop. On phones, use a full-width map with more compact spacing.
- Put guidance after the map and provide a prominent save action at the end of the form.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `ongoing-meeting-availability` covers the editor layout and draft protection.

## Impact

The hosting editor, its browser navigation, and its tests change.
