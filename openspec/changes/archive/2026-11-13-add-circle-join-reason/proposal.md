# Add a circle join reason

## Why

Members can capture why a circle matters to them when they join.

## What Changes

- Offer an optional single-line reason when joining a circle on the web.
- Store the reason on the member's circle membership and remove it when they leave.
- Show the reason on the member's Circles tab to signed-in viewers who can access the profile.

## Impact

The circle join flow and membership API.
