# Clarify counts in the public Experience summary

## Why

Profile summaries mix counts, percentages and omitted answers. Exact counts
make small samples easier to read and let members see when an answer was not
recorded. This is part of #3750.

## What changes

- Use counts of public Experiences for recommendations, reported interactions
  and the existing gender breakdown.
- Show missing recommendations and interactions explicitly. Keep the scope
  of the gender breakdown visible where it omits other or unlisted values.
- Preserve the existing minimum list sizes for the detailed summaries.
