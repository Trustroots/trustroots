## Why

Support team members need to inspect the profile content of hidden accounts during support work.

## What Changes

- Let Support team members open hidden member profiles from the Support member page using the existing profile view.
- Keep member-facing restrictions, blocks and administrator-only moderation tools in place.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `admin-moderation`: Support team access to hidden profile content.

## Impact

The users profile read policy and admin Support member page change. No migration is required.
