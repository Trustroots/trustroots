## Why

Members have to scroll past their connections to see featured updates, and the connections map does not show people who are available to meet without hosting.

## What Changes

- Place Trustroots updates first on the signed-in homepage, followed by the connections map and recently active connections.
- Use a consistent Trustroots visual treatment for the three areas.
- Show approximate meeting locations for eligible connections who have set their ongoing meeting availability to yes and are not hosting.

## Impact

- Affected specs: site-presentation
- Affected code: homepage, connections discovery, shared map markers
