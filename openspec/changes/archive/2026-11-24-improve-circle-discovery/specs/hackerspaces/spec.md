## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Compact Hackers circle layout

The Hackers circle SHALL place wiki resources after member discovery at desktop
and mobile widths. The Circle Wiki action SHALL appear with the attributed
resources. Both map circle headers SHALL use a compact hero with a fading photo
background so following content can appear in the initial desktop viewport.

#### Scenario: Member browses the Hackers circle on desktop

- **WHEN** a circle member opens the Hackers circle
- **THEN** member groups precede the wiki resources below the map hero
- **AND** the Circle Wiki button appears beside the resource attribution

#### Scenario: Member browses the Hackers circle on mobile

- **WHEN** a circle member opens the Hackers circle on a narrow viewport
- **THEN** the wiki resources appear below the member groups
