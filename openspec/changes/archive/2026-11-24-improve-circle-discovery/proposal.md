## Why

The circle catalogue is hard to scan, and recently created circles can appear
below established ones. Circle detail pages give the wiki links space ahead of
member discovery.

## What Changes

- Add a filter at the top of the circle catalogue that updates as people type.
- List circles marked new by the API above other circles.
- Place circle wiki links below member discovery, including the attributed
  resources on the Hackers circle page.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `circles`: Catalogue filtering, new-circle placement, and wiki-link placement.
- `hackerspaces`: Display the attributed resources after member discovery.

## Impact

The web catalogue and detail pages change. The existing API supplies the new
circle marker and continues to control circle visibility.
