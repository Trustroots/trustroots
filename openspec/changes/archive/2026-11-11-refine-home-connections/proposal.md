## Why

The fixed navigation can cover the homepage heading, activity labels sit outside their cards, and the eight-card limit leaves a gap in a three-column grid. The hosting popup also runs the member name into the offer link.

## What Changes

- Show up to nine recent connections in aligned, responsive cards with activity inside each card.
- Position the recent connections and hosting map below the fixed navigation.
- Give the hosting popup a separate, clearly labelled offer link below the member name.

## Impact

- Affected specs: site-presentation
- Affected code: signed-in homepage, connections discovery endpoint and location map popups
