## Why

Support receives an alert when a member exceeds the messaging recipient limit.
Reviewing the messages that triggered the alert helps Support investigate it.

## What Changes

- Store the sender and exact throttle window for each new Support alert review.
- Link the alert email to a paginated, audited review of outgoing messages.
- Use the existing Support and administrator roles for the review.

## Impact

The messaging throttle, Support alert email and moderation tools gain a scoped
review workflow. Existing verified member reports keep their conversation view.
Historical alerts do not gain review links.
