## Why

New visitors need a clear place to find the Trustroots starter guide, profile
advice, safety guidance and background on hospitality exchange.

## What Changes

- Add a Get Started category to the FAQ sidebar with four questions and
  links to the relevant Trustroots Wiki articles.
- Include its questions and answers in the existing FAQ search.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `public-access`: Visitors can browse and search the Get Started FAQ.

## Impact

The public FAQ gains a page at `/faq/get-started`.
