## Why

Members currently have to find a save button and leave the hosting editor to
persist changes. Profile actions also give little guidance about missing setup.

## What Changes

- Automatically persist valid edits in the web hosting editor.
- Guide profile members without hosting coordinates to the Location tab before
  continuing to meetups.
- Show private reminders for missing About me descriptions and profile photos
  on the member's own profile and in the hosting editor.

## Impact

- Affects the offers and users modules.
- The host create response adds the created ID. Existing clients and data remain
  compatible and no migration is required.
