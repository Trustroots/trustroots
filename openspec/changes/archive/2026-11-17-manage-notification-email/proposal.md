## Why

Members need control over the volume of optional Trustroots emails, especially when they already read messages in the app.

## What Changes

- Offer immediate reminders, daily or weekly digests, and an off setting for unread-message emails.
- Add a separate hosting reminder toggle and a signed unsubscribe link for each optional category.
- Preserve message delivery and push alerts independently of email choices.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `member-notifications`: Member-controlled optional email frequency and unsubscribe.
