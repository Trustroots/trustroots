## Why

A member who is blocked cannot share an independent account or access their own Experience and report action on the blocker's profile unless a reciprocal Experience already exists.

## What Changes

- Provide limited blocked-profile access for writing and viewing the viewer's own Experience and reporting the member.
- Keep other profile details and Experiences hidden.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `experiences-references`: Allow blocked members to share and view their own Experience and reach support.

## Impact

The experiences and users modules and the minimal recipient API change. Existing data needs no migration; existing submission and publication rules remain in effect.
