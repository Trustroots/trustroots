## Why

Member previews show different identity fields and profile controls across Trustroots clients. A common layout makes people easier to recognise as members move between discovery, maps, contacts and conversations.

## What Changes

- Standardise avatar, display name, username and profile affordances across the web, Android and iOS.
- Reuse a compact identity component within each client for member previews and keep screen-specific metadata and actions alongside it.
- Verify discovery, contacts and offers with representative fixtures and capture before-and-after screenshots.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `member-profiles` gains a consistent presentation contract for member identity previews.

## Impact

This affects member-facing UI in the web discovery, profiles, contacts, offers, messages and Experiences modules and their Android and iOS counterparts. Existing API responses, member visibility, routes and stored data support the change.
