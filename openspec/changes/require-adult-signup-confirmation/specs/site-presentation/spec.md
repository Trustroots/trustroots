## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Adult membership wording

The rules, general FAQ, privacy page and signup form SHALL state that Trustroots is for people aged 18 or older. General FAQ wording about who can sign up SHALL reflect this age requirement.

#### Scenario: Visitor checks eligibility

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the rules, general FAQ, privacy page or signup form
- **THEN** the page states the minimum age of 18
